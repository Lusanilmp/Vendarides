Privacy information (draft)

VendaRides uses contact details, account information, trip requests, bookings and safety reports to provide its services. Private information should only be accessed by authorised people. Location sharing requires consent and can be stopped.

Before public launch, the operator must publish a complete POPIA-compliant privacy notice identifying the responsible party, contact details, retention periods, recipients, rights and complaint procedure.

This is a pre-launch draft, not a completed legal policy.
